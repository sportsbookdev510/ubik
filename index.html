  2. 2017

06 / Questions

Short answers.

Who is Tigrino?+

A male wild cat, about 10 years old and 3 lbs, living at La Senda Verde Wildlife Sanctuary in Bolivia's Yungas since 2017. In September 2026 scientists confirmed he belongs to Leopardus tilcayo, a species never described before. He is the only known individual.

What is Leopardus tilcayo?+

A small wild tiger cat species from Bolivia's Yungas cloud forest, described on September 17, 2026 in Current Biology and announced by National Geographic. The first new living cat species named since the pampas cat in 1923.

Tigrino vs tilcayo?+

Tigrino is the cat's name, given by sanctuary staff in 2017. Tilcayo is the species name, from the word Yungas locals have always used for the animal. One cat, two names.

How much has been donated?+

Over $100,000 within 48 hours, to La Senda Verde Animal Refuge Inc, the sanctuary's US 501(c)(3). In their words it goes to “Tigrino and more than 1,000 rescued animals.” The counter on this page reads directly from donate.gg. The sanctuary confirmed receipt on X, in a photo, and on video.

What is $TIGRINO?+

A community-run memecoin on Robinhood Chain, tradeable on Uniswap as of September 18, 2026. 100% of its creator fees are locked to the sanctuary; the creator receives 0% and holds no tokens. No team, no roadmap, no affiliation with National Geographic or the study authors. The price can go to zero.

Is this the sanctuary's website?+

No. This is a community site. The sanctuary is sendaverde.org and @La_Senda_Verde on X. They know about this project and have publicly acknowledged it, but they don't run it.
